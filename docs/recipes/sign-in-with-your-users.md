# Sign in with your own users

Let your application's users into the backend.

```dotenv
WAITLIST_KIT_AUTH=host
WAITLIST_KIT_ADMIN_EMAILS="owner@example.org, marketing@example.org"
```

Or define the abilities with your own roles; the kit never overrides a definition you made. See [Backend](../tools/backend.md#abilities).
